Mahjong

Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Mahjong is a game you play on your own device against computer opponents. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: it does not collect any.

What the app collects

Nothing. Mahjong does not collect, transmit or share any personal information. The app:

  • has no accounts and never asks who you are;
  • makes no network connections of its own;
  • contains no advertising;
  • contains no analytics or tracking tools;
  • includes no third-party software that collects data.

One link in the app, "Buy an official card", opens the National Mah Jongg League's website in your web browser when you tap it. What happens on that website is covered by the League's own policies.

What is stored on your device

To work properly, the app saves a small amount of information on your device only:

  • The scorepad and the hand in progress, so you can pick up where you left off.
  • Hand history: for each hand you finish, the date, the card played, who won and with which hand, and what each player won or paid. This is what the History screen uses.
  • Settings: sound on or off, the card you chose, the Charleston setting, the time to call, and whether you have already seen the instructions.
  • A note of what you have bought, so locked features open right away before Apple confirms your purchases.

This information never leaves your device through the app, and the developer cannot see it. If you back up your device with Apple's own backup services, it may be included in that backup under Apple's terms.

Purchases

The app offers optional purchases and optional tips. These are handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. The app never sees your name, payment details or address; it only learns from Apple which purchases your account owns.

Deleting your information

Clearing the scorepad on the start menu resets the running scores. Deleting the app from your device deletes everything it has stored.

Children

Mahjong does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what is described here, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
