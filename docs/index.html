How to play

The app has full instructions under How to play on the start menu and in the game menu. The rules in brief:

  • Be the first to make one of the hands on the card: 14 tiles that match a line exactly.
  • Before play, everyone passes tiles in the Charleston: right, across, left, an optional second Charleston, then a courtesy pass with the player across. Jokers are never passed.
  • On your turn you draw a tile and discard one. Tap a tile to pick it, and tap it again to discard it.
  • Every discard waits in the middle for a moment. If you need it for a pung, kong or quint, call it; you can only call a tile for a single or a pair when it gives you Mahjong.
  • Jokers stand in for any tile in a group of three or more. On your turn, tap a glowing joker in anyone's exposed group to swap your real tile for it.
  • When your hand is complete, tap Mahjong! Everyone pays you the hand's value; the discarder pays double, a tile you drew yourself makes everyone pay double, and a hand with no jokers counts double.

Common questions

How do I see which tile is which?

Press and hold any tile, anywhere on the screen. It appears large, with its name.

How do I see the hands on the card?

During a hand, tap Card. The hands are listed closest first, and the tiles you still need are dimmed. Tap a hand to see it large. Swipe sideways to turn the page. From the start menu, tap the card's name and then Hands.

Do I need my own card?

To play a National Mah Jongg League card you need your own official card from the League, which you can buy at nationalmahjonggleague.org. Buying the printed card from the League does not unlock it in this app, and buying it in this app does not send you a printed card: you need both. The practice card is written for this game and is always free. This app is independent and is not affiliated with the National Mah Jongg League.

What do the purchases include?

  • This year's card lets you play the current year's card. Next year's card is sold separately. Past years' cards are free.
  • The full game includes this year's card, plus your hand history and stats, playing without the Charleston, the wall and dice (roll for East, break the wall and deal from it), and a choice of how long each discard can be called.
  • If you already own this year's card, the upgrade adds the rest of the full game for less.
  • Tips are optional and unlock nothing extra.

I bought something but it is locked again.

On the start menu tap Unlock full game, then Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

What is the scorepad?

Each hand is a game of its own. The scorepad keeps a running total across hands, like the one on your table, until you tap Clear on the start menu. Your history keeps every hand even after you clear the scorepad.

Does the game wait while I look at the menu or the card?

Yes. The computer players wait while any menu or panel is open.

The phone does not vibrate.

Haptics follow your iPhone's settings: check Settings, then Sounds & Haptics, then System Haptics. iPads do not have haptics.
